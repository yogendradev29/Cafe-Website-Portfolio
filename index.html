About Us

A neighborhood cafe with a roastery soul.

Established in 2024, bringing artisanal roasts and fresh bakes to our neighborhood. The Pink City Roasters pairs premium beans with Jaipur's easy warmth, creating a space where college projects, client calls, first dates, and coffee rituals all feel at home.
